# Aster PX100 — Fictional pump monitoring guide

This document is a synthetic portfolio example, not a real manufacturer's manual.

## Vibration monitoring
The Aster PX100 vibration alert threshold is 4.5 mm/s RMS. The monitoring service raises an alert after three consecutive readings above this threshold. A reading equal to the threshold does not count as an exceedance. Sampling occurs once every 10 seconds.

## Temperature monitoring
The Aster PX100 bearing temperature warning threshold is 75 degrees Celsius. The temperature channel uses a separate sensor from the vibration channel. A temperature warning is recorded in the maintenance event log.

## Maintenance records
For an Aster PX100 maintenance ticket, record the asset identifier, event timestamp, measured vibration, and bearing temperature. The guide does not specify purchase prices, warranty periods, spare-part prices, or a replacement schedule.
